Restaurant & Bar

A proper meal, and a place to unwind

The restaurant serves guests and events with sit-down meals and full catering, and the adjoining bar is stocked for a quiet evening in after a long drive.

On the menu

What's on offer

Meals are prepared for guests staying at the lodge as well as outside groups booking the space for an event. The kitchen also handles catering for conferences held on site.

  • Sit-down meals for guests and groups
  • Full catering for events on site
  • A stocked bar for the evening
