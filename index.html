About Ondjombo

Welcome to Ondjombo Lodge

Located on the C35, Omakange / Kamanjab Main Road in Ruacana, Ondjombo Lodge offers comfortable accommodation, conference facilities and quality dining, making it the ideal stop for business travellers, tour groups and visitors exploring Northern Namibia.
