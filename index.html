About Ondjombo

A quiet stop on the road to the far north

Ondjombo Lodge sits on the Omakange Main Road, C38, in Ruacana, Omusati Region, right along the route to some of Northern Namibia's most iconic attractions. It's a straightforward, comfortable base for leisure travellers, corporate visitors and groups passing through the region.

The lodge combines sixteen guest rooms with a restaurant and catering service, dedicated conference facilities, and secure parking, all within a peaceful setting away from the main road.

Accommodation

Sixteen rooms, kept simple

Every room at Ondjombo is a twin-bed room with its own ensuite bathroom. Nothing elaborate, just a clean, well-kept place to rest before the next stretch of road, whether you are travelling for business, leisure or as part of a larger group.

  • Twin beds in every room
  • Private ensuite bathroom
  • Wi-Fi access throughout the lodge

Around Ruacana

Explore Northern Namibia

Ondjombo sits along the route to some of the region's best-known attractions, an easy base for exploring before or after a longer trip north.

Waterfall

Ruacana Falls

One of Africa's largest waterfalls by volume, formed where the Kunene River drops into a 120-metre gorge on the Angola border.

River Route

Kunene River & Epupa Falls

Follow the river route north to Epupa, where the Kunene splits into cascades through baobab and palm-lined gorges.

Culture

Himba Cultural Experiences

Guided visits into Kaokoland introduce the traditions of the OvaHimba, one of Namibia's last semi-nomadic peoples.

Border

Angola Border Crossing

Ruacana sits directly on the crossing into Angola, a natural stop for travellers or businesses moving further north.
