Accommodation

Sixteen rooms, kept simple

Every room at Ondjombo is a twin-bed room with its own ensuite bathroom. Nothing elaborate, just a clean, well-kept place to rest before the next stretch of road, whether you are travelling for business, leisure or as part of a larger group.

Room details

What's included

Rooms are laid out for straightforward, comfortable stays: twin beds, a private bathroom, and the basics done well. The lodge sits away from the main road, so nights are quiet.

  • Twin beds in every room
  • Private ensuite bathroom
  • Wi-Fi access throughout the lodge
  • Secure, gated parking on site

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 10:00. Earlier check-in or later check-out can usually be arranged on request, subject to room availability. [CONFIRM WITH CLIENT]

Can rooms be booked for groups?

Yes. With sixteen rooms on site, Ondjombo can accommodate tour groups, teams and larger travelling parties. Contact the lodge directly to check availability and arrange a group rate. [CONFIRM WITH CLIENT]

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast is available each morning through the lodge restaurant. Whether it is bundled into the room rate or charged separately depends on the package booked. [CONFIRM WITH CLIENT]

What is the cancellation policy?

Reservations can generally be amended or cancelled with reasonable notice ahead of arrival. Get in touch when booking to confirm the exact terms and any deposit requirements. [CONFIRM WITH CLIENT]
