Around Ruacana

Explore Northern Namibia

Ondjombo sits along the route to some of the region's best-known attractions, an easy base for exploring before or after a longer trip north.

Waterfall

Ruacana Falls

One of Africa's largest waterfalls by volume, formed where the Kunene River drops into a 120-metre gorge on the Angola border. The falls run strongest during and just after the rainy season, when the Kunene is high, and reduce to a trickle once the dam upstream regulates flow in drier months.

About 5 km · 10 minute drive from the lodge
River Route

Kunene River & Epupa Falls

Follow the river route west to Epupa, where the Kunene splits into a series of cascades through gorges lined with baobab and makalani palm. It is a longer, mostly gravel drive, better suited to a full day trip or an overnight stop rather than a quick out-and-back.

Roughly 180 km · 3 to 4 hour drive [CONFIRM WITH CLIENT]
Culture

Himba Cultural Experiences

Guided visits into Kaokoland introduce the traditions of the OvaHimba, one of Namibia's last semi-nomadic peoples, known for their ochre-dyed skin and cattle-herding way of life. Visits are best arranged through a local guide who can make the right introductions to a homestead.

Distance depends on route [CONFIRM WITH CLIENT]
Border

Angola Border Crossing

Ruacana sits directly on the crossing into Angola, a natural stop for travellers heading further north or businesses moving goods across the border. Ondjombo makes a convenient overnight stop the night before or after crossing.

About 2 km · 5 minute drive from the lodge
